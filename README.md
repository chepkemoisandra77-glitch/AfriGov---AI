# AfriGov---AI
A dashboard tracking AI governance gaps across 54 African countries
A real time interactive dashboard exposing the AI governance gap across all 54 nations - tracking national AI strategies, data protection laws, regulatory readiness, and inclusion scores.

## Why I built this
Africa is home to over 1.4 billion people, yet African voices are almost entirely absent from global AI governance discussions. Most AI systems are built without African data, Africn languages or African priorities in mind.
This project maps exactly where the gaps are in each country so that researchers and advocates have a clear picture of what needs to change before the governance window closes.

#What it tracks 
National AI strategy 
Data protection legislation
Regulatory capacity
Technical capacity
Digital inclusion

#Builts with
HTML,CSS,Javascript (no frameworks)
Data sourced from ITU, UNCTAD,African Union AI strategy and national government portals
